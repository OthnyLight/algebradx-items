# Changelog

## v1.0.0 (2026-10-10)
First release.
- 40 original items in 8 strands (5 each); 120 distractors coded to a 64-entry misconception catalog with teaching moves, probes and research references.
- Parallel 20-item Forms A and B; teacher guide; CSV/JSON exports; answer key with misconception codes.
- Local calibration kit (`calibration/calibrate.py`), standard library only; synthetic demo data for testing.
- Validation: 143 of 143 machine-checkable options pass exact-arithmetic key checks; 17 verbal options reviewed by hand.
- Items drafted with AI assistance (Claude, Anthropic) from the published research; reviewed and verified by the author on 2026-10-10.
- Deferred to v1.1: item-level NAEP alignment (`docs/NAEP_ALIGNMENT.csv`).
